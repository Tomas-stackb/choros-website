Your showroom is expensive. Fill it on purpose.

Choros runs Google Ads, Meta Ads, local SEO and instant follow-up for kitchen and bathroom showrooms across the UK, measured in booked design consultations. A fitted kitchen starts as a Tuesday-night search; we make sure it ends in your showroom.

The problem is rarely the ads. It is what happens after the click.

Showroom projects are high-ticket and slow-burning. Homeowners research for weeks, often enquire with more than one showroom, and book a design consultation with the one that responds properly. If your enquiries land in an inbox that gets checked between appointments, you are paying to generate leads for whoever replies first.

We build the system that responds in minutes, nurtures the slow burners for months, and books consultations into your designers’ calendars. Then we point ad spend at it.
