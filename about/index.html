A software, AI and marketing team in Carmarthen, working UK-wide.

Choros is a software, AI and marketing company founded in Carmarthen in 2024. We build bespoke platforms and AI systems, run our own products in production, and look after the client systems in our care, including HedgePoint, the FX risk platform at hedgepoint.app.

What we believe about building software

Software should cost what it takes to build, not what it takes to administer. The people who write the code should be senior enough to own it in production. If a system is not worth running for years, it was not worth building.
