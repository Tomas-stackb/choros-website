About

Founded 2024, Carmarthen. Working UK-wide.

Choros is an AI-native software, AI and marketing team. Small and senior on purpose, with our own products in production and our clients’ systems in our care.

What we believe about building software

Software should cost what it takes to build, not what it takes to administer. The people who write the code should be senior enough to own it in production. If a system is not worth running for years, it was not worth building.
