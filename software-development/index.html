Questions about a build

What does a build cost?

Builds are fixed scope and fixed price: you get a written specification and a number before we start. HedgePoint was delivered in four months for roughly a tenth of a £1M quote.

Who owns the code?

You do. It is written into the agreement.

What if you get hit by a bus?

Everything is documented and in your repository. If you ever want to take it in-house or move to another supplier, you can, and we will help.

Do we have to keep you on for maintenance?

No. Maintenance is optional and separate.

Do we have to commit to a big project to start?

No. You can start with the AI Operations Audit, which is a defined piece of work with a defined price. What happens after that is your decision.
