Straight answers

Questions buyers actually ask

How can you be so much cheaper than the quotes we have had?

Because we are structured differently. Senior engineers using AI tooling throughout the build, no management layer, and we scope to production rather than to a discovery process. The output is not cheaper software, it is the same software without the overhead.

Who owns the code?

You do. It is written into the agreement.

What if you get hit by a bus?

Everything is documented and in your repository. If you ever want to take it in-house or move to another supplier, you can, and we will help.

Do we have to commit to a big project to start?

No. Most clients start with an audit, which is a defined piece of work with a defined price. What happens after that is your decision.

We are not a tech company. Is this for us?

Most of our clients are not tech companies. They are distributors, hospitality groups, financial services firms and trade businesses.

Two weeks, a fixed price, and a written answer on where AI and automation would actually pay for themselves in your business. No obligation to build anything with us.
