Questions buyers ask us

How can you be so much cheaper than the quotes we have had?

You get the same software without the overhead: senior engineers using AI tooling throughout the build, no management layer, and a price for the build rather than for a discovery process. HedgePoint is the clearest example: quoted around £1M elsewhere, delivered for roughly a tenth.

What does it cost?

The AI Operations Audit is from £4,950, fixed, and delivered in two weeks. Builds are fixed scope and fixed price: you get a written specification and a number before we start.

Who owns the code?

You do. It is written into the agreement.

What if you get hit by a bus?

Everything is documented and in your repository. If you ever want to take it in-house or move to another supplier, you can, and we will help.

Do we have to commit to a big project to start?

No. You can start with the AI Operations Audit, which is a defined piece of work with a defined price. What happens after that is your decision.

We are not a tech company. Is this for us?

Probably. The AI Operations Audit starts with how your business runs, not with your technology, and builds are fixed scope and fixed price with a written specification you can read. We also run marketing for trades, showrooms and hospitality.

What happens after I book a call?

You choose a time in the calendar, then talk to the team about what you are trying to build, automate or fix. We tell you whether it is worth doing. If it is, you get a written specification and a price before anything is built.
