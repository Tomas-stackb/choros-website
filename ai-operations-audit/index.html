Who it is for

Established UK businesses that want to know where AI and automation would pay for themselves before they pay to build anything.

It is a review of your operation, not of your technology: where time and headcount are going, and what is realistically possible with your systems, data and integrations. We have shipped in financial services, retail and distribution and hospitality.

What happens after the audit

You decide. If the numbers justify a build, we build and run it: process automation, document and data extraction, customer-facing AI where it improves the experience, internal tooling, computer vision, and forecasting and pricing systems.

If the return is not there, we tell you, and that is a legitimate outcome.
