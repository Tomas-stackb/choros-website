Case study — Retail & distribution, Swansea

Three full-time staff, returned to the work they were hired for, in three weeks.

Curveball’s team were watching competitor listings and repricing by hand, every day. We built the system that does it for them.

3 weeks
From brief to live
3 staff
Back to distribution, where the business needed them
3 marketplaces
Amazon, eBay and CeX, watched continuously

The situation

Curveball, a Swansea reseller, had three people employed full time doing one thing: watching competitor listings on Amazon, eBay and CeX, spotting lines that were in demand and out of stock elsewhere, and raising prices by hand.

It worked. It was also three salaries spent on something a machine should do, and it only ever ran at the speed a human could refresh a page.

What we built

A pricing automation tool that reads competitor price, discount and stock-level data across the three marketplaces and adjusts Curveball’s pricing automatically, against rules the business controls.

Where three people could check a listing a few times a day, the system watches continuously and reacts the moment a competitor’s stock position changes.

The result

Live in three weeks. All three staff returned to distribution, where they had come from and where the business needed them. The repricing did not stop; the salaries spent on it did.
