Stock was disappearing. We made it visible.

AI vision in the physical world: smart cameras tied into stock management at the restaurant and sky bar at the top of Wales’ tallest building.

The situation

Altitude 28 operates a restaurant and sky bar on the top floors of the Meridian Tower in Swansea. Stock at the site was going missing, and nobody could say where it was going.

Without knowing where it was going, there was no way to fix it. Every conversation about the problem was a conversation about opinions.

What we built

Smart cameras running AI vision recognition, integrated with stock management, so movement and allocation could be seen rather than guessed at. Stock leaves a location, the system knows; stock arrives somewhere it should not be, the system knows that too.

This is the same engineering capability we apply to platforms and automation, pointed at a physical operation. Dashboards are the easy part. This job needed cameras, a stockroom and a bar in service.

The result

The cause stopped being a matter of opinion. The business could see where stock moved and where it went wrong, and decisions about fixing it became decisions about facts.
