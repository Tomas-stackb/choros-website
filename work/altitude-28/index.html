Case study — Hospitality, Swansea

Stock was disappearing. We made it visible.

AI vision in the physical world: smart cameras tied into stock management at the restaurant and sky bar at the top of Wales’ tallest building.

The situation

Altitude 28 operates a restaurant and sky bar on the top floors of the Meridian Tower in Swansea. Stock at the site was going missing, and nobody could say whether it was theft, loss, or simply being distributed and allocated wrongly.

Without knowing which, there was no way to fix it. Every conversation about the problem was a conversation about opinions.

What we built

Smart cameras running AI vision recognition, integrated with stock management, so movement and allocation could be seen rather than guessed at. Stock leaves a location, the system knows; stock arrives somewhere it should not be, the system knows that too.

This is the same engineering capability we apply to platforms and automation, pointed at a physical operation. Dashboards are easy; cameras, stockrooms and a working bar are where most AI suppliers stop.

The result

The cause stopped being a matter of opinion. The business could see where stock moved and where it went wrong, and decisions about fixing it became decisions about facts.
