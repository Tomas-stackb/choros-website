An FX risk platform quoted at £1M, built for about a tenth in four months.

HedgePoint is a full FX treasury risk management platform. We designed it, built it, and we still secure and maintain it today.

The situation

The client needed a platform to manage foreign exchange risk for their own customers. A traditional development house quoted approximately £1M over eighteen months.

At that price and timeline the product would have been obsolete before launch, and the business case did not survive contact with the quote. The choice was to abandon the product, or find a team structured to build it differently.

What we built

A full FX treasury risk management platform: exposure tracking, hedging positions, settlement workflows and reporting, delivered as a secure multi-tenant web application. Designed, architected and built by us, end to end.

We shipped in short cycles with working software in front of the client throughout, and went to market with more functionality than the original £1M scope described.

The result

Delivered in four months. Roughly a tenth of the original quote. Shipped with more features than the original scope included.

The platform now serves major UK businesses managing real FX exposure, among them a national supermarket chain, a champagne house and two major travel businesses.

What we still do

We still run HedgePoint in production: hosting, security, monitoring, maintenance and new features.
